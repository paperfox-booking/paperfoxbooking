# Helping customers

Common questions, and how to sort them out in the system.

## "Have you received my payment?"

Open **People** (or the event's **People booked** list), search for them,
and open their booking. **Payments** shows everything recorded. If the
money is in the bank but not recorded, record it: they'll get the right
email (B2, B3 or a thank you).

## "How much do I still owe, and when?"

Their booking's page shows the total, what's paid, what's left and the
balance due date. Send them the **balance reminder** from **Send an email**:
it has the amount, the date and the bank details with their reference.

## "I didn't get the email."

On their booking, **Emails sent** shows what was sent, when, and to which
address, including any that couldn't be sent and why. Check the address
(fix it on the people booked list), ask them to look in their spam folder,
then send it again from **Send an email**.

## "Can I change from the 4pm start to the 9am start?"

Change their **Option** on the people booked list. Their price and deposit
follow the new option (unless they had a special price, which is kept: use
**Change the price** on their booking if it needs changing). Then send a
balance reminder so they know what's left to pay.

## "I can't come any more."

Follow the terms (shown in Settings): more than six weeks before, the £60
admin fee is kept; within six weeks, nothing is refunded unless the place is
resold. **Cancel this booking** on their page, with the reason. The
**Refunds** section then shows what they've paid, how long before the event
it is, and the terms' own words. Make any refund by bank transfer, then
**Record a refund**. Offer the place to the first person on the **waiting
list**.

## "Can my friend come instead?"

The terms need Paper Fox's written approval. If you agree, open the
booking and choose **Pass this place to someone else**. Start typing the
friend's name to pick them if they've been before, or type their name and
email, and say who approved it. The booking moves to them with its
reference, option, room and payments, and the reason goes in its notes.
They settle up between themselves. Then send them B2 or B3 from **Send an
email**, so they have the details.

## "Can I sit with my friend?"

Put it in their **Notes** on the people booked list. The seating plan will
use these once it's built.

## "What's included? Where do I park? When do I arrive?"

All in the confirmation PDF and the joining details email, from the event's
own details. Keep **What's included**, **Hotel information** and **Joining
details** up to date under the event's **Change details**.

## "Can I have a receipt?"

Open their booking and choose **Download a statement of their payments**.
The PDF lists every payment and refund, what they've paid and what's left,
so you can email it to them. Once customers can sign in, they can download
it themselves from their account.
