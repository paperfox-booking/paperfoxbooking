# Paper Fox's current emails

These are the three standard emails Paper Fox sends today from Outlook. They are the starting point for the system's email templates. They're copied word for word from the March 2027 versions (spelling included). Customer names and addresses are left out.

The attachments they mention:
- `Paper Fox Events Terms & Conditions 2026.pdf`, kept here as [`terms-and-conditions-2026.pdf`](terms-and-conditions-2026.pdf).
- `2 - March 27 Confirmation.pdf`, kept here as [`march-2027-confirmation.pdf`](march-2027-confirmation.pdf).
- `1-Mar 27 booking details.pdf`. This is **not** kept here because it contains the bank details. It gives the event date, the venue, prices and deposits, payment due dates, bank details, hotel rates and contacts, and a venue map.

## B1: booking received

**Subject:** March 2027 Paper Fox (B1)

**Attachments:** booking details PDF, terms and conditions PDF

> We are delighted to confirm that we have received your booking form and we have reserved a place for you on the March 2027 event.
>
> The price for the weekend is £195 or to start your weekend at 9am and have extra scrappy time it is £245.
>
> Please find the following attachments:
>
> - The Paper Fox Escape brochure contains all the information you need to know at time of booking including payment information
> - Booking terms and conditions. Please read through these carefully.
>
> Your place is reserved for a short period of time and only is confirmed when we receive your deposit or full payment. This is due before the 30th March 2026. After this date your place will be released.
>
> You have two deposit options :-
>
> - If you wish to attend from 4pm Friday to 5pm Sunday then the deposit is £60
> - If you wish to start your weekend scrapping at 9am Friday with lunch, class kit and other goodies then we require a deposit of £85
>
> You can book a hotel room after Friday 13th March 2027 direct with the hotel to get our rate. All the details are on the attached document. We highly recommend you do this as soon as possible as they only hold our rate for a limited about of time. Please make sure you book the room in the name required as duplicate names will likely result in the room being cancelled (so if you book a room for someone else in your name as an example).
>
> IMPORTANT – Please read the terms and conditions as deposits are non-refundable under any circumstance unless of course we have to cancel your place or the event.
>
> What happens next?
>
> - When your payment is received, you will receive a confirmation email. This confirms that your place is secured. including details of a private Facebook group. Don't worry if you are not on Facebook - you still receive important information via email
> - Six weeks before the start of the event, the balancing payment is due.
> - Approx. four weeks before our crafting weekend, we will contact you regarding optional self-paced class kits. Delegates may purchase a kit before they go on general sale for the public
> - A few days before the event, you will receive an email with full details of the everything you need to know for a great weekend away. It includes arrival, timetable for the weekend, local amenities plus typical FAQs. This should cover any questions you may have, but if not, feel free to pop us an email
>
> It is important to us that you have a relaxed enjoyable weekend so we will keep you informed along the way.
>
> Thank you again for choosing Paper Fox. We are looking forward to spending a weekend of crafting with you.
