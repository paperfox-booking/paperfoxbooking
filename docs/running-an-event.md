# Running an event

A guide for the Paper Fox team. It grows as each part of the system is
built. Sign in at **Team sign in** (the link at the bottom of every page),
then use the menu at the top of the admin area.

## Making next year's (or next quarter's) event

The quickest way is to copy an event you've already set up:

1. Go to **Events** and open the event to copy, for example March 2027.
2. Choose **Copy this event**.
3. Check the name and start date. We suggest the same weekday a year on (so
   a Friday stays a Friday), and move the month and year in the name to
   match. Change them if you're making, say, June from March.
4. Choose **Copy event**.

The copy is a **draft**, so only the team can see it. Every date moves on by
the same number of days: the start and end, the balance due date and when
booking opens. Start times stay the same even when the clocks change, so a
9am start stays 9am.

The copy has the same options, prices and deposits, description, what's
included and hotel information. Two things to do before publishing:

- add the new event's **Facebook group** (each event has its own, so it's
  never copied);
- check the **hotel room** rates, which often change.

## Adding an event from scratch

Go to **Events → Add an event**. Picking the start date fills in the end date
(2 days later) and the balance due date (6 weeks before). You can change
either.

Leave **Add the usual two options** ticked to add:

- **Weekend (4pm start):** £195, with a £60 deposit;
- **Friday Kick Starter (9am start):** £245, with an £85 deposit.

## Options, prices and deposits

Open the event and choose **Change** under an option to change its price,
deposit or start time. Changing a price only affects new bookings.

- **Deposit:** what's paid to secure a place. The rest is due by the event's
  balance due date. Leave it empty if people pay in full.
- **On sale:** untick to stop people booking that option for a while.
- **Remove this option:** at the bottom of the option's page, if it's no
  longer offered at all.

## The balance due date

Each event has its own balance due date, 6 weeks before by default. Anyone
booking on or after that date pays in full. Change it under **Change
details**: for example, March 2027's is Tuesday 26 January 2027.

## Showing an event on the website

An event starts as a draft. At the bottom of the event's page, under **On the
website**:

- **Publish** shows it on the website. It needs at least one option on sale.
- **Mark as sold out** keeps it on the website, marked as sold out, with a
  note to email you to join the waiting list.
- **Reopen bookings** undoes sold out.
- **Unpublish** takes it off the website again.
- **Cancel this event** shows it as cancelled. This can't be undone.
- **Delete this draft** removes a draft you don't need.

Changes show on the website straight away.

## Venues

Go to **Venues** to change the Holiday Inn's details (address, phone, map
link, parking and access) or add another venue. These show on each event's
page.

## Bringing in a booking spreadsheet

For an event that already has bookings on a spreadsheet, like March 2027:

1. Make sure the event exists, with its options (the 4pm and 9am starts).
2. Open the event, choose **People booked**, then **Import a spreadsheet**.
3. Choose the Excel file (.xlsx) and **Check the spreadsheet**. Nothing is
   saved yet. The preview shows:
   - how many people, totals due, received and still owed, and the rooms;
   - anything about the whole sheet, such as totals that don't add up;
   - **rows to check**, such as a 9am person charged the 4pm price, a row
     with no balance formula, or two names in one row;
   - rows that weren't imported (for example the waiting list below the
     totals).
4. Choose **Import**. Everyone is saved together, or nobody is.

The rows marked "AA" are put on the team and listed first. What each person
has paid so far is brought across as one payment, and the last email in
"Booking Sent" goes into their emails. Imports only work on an event with
nobody booked yet, so nobody can be added twice.

## The people booked list

Open an event and choose **People booked**. It works like the spreadsheet:

- **Type into any box** to change it. It saves when you move on. Enter and
  the up and down arrows move between rows, and Escape undoes what you
  typed.
- **Recording bank transfers:** type the amount in **Pay today** and press
  Enter. It's recorded as a bank transfer received today, and the cursor
  moves to the next person, so you can work down your bank statement. The
  grey number in the box is what's needed next (the rest of the deposit, or
  the balance).
- **Cash, another date or a note:** choose **Add** next to what they've
  received.
- **Find someone** searches names, emails, references and notes. Tick **Only
  people who owe money** or **Only overdue** to see who to chase.
- The boxes at the top show places, options, rooms (against each room's
  limit) and money: total due, received, still owed and overdue.
- **Add someone** at the bottom. Start typing a name to pick someone who's
  been before.
- **Download as a spreadsheet (CSV)** gives you the list for printing or
  Excel.

Door staff see who's booked and their details, but not money.

## A booking's own page

Choose someone's reference (like PF-2027-0001) to open their booking. It
shows what they owe and have paid, and:

- **Payments:** every payment, who recorded it, and **Remove** for one
  recorded by mistake (with a reason; it stays in the history);
- **Record a payment** with the amount, date, how it was paid and a note;
- **Emails sent:** including ones from the spreadsheet, and **Record an
  email sent outside the system**;
- **Change the price,** with a reason (for example a discount);
- **Cancel this booking** (with a reason), which frees up the place, or
  **Reinstate** it;
- **History:** every change, when and by whom.

## Everyone on file

**People** in the menu lists everyone who's booked, kept from one event to
the next. It's a spreadsheet too: type into it to update details for lots of
people at once. Choose **Profile** for someone's details and all their
bookings.

## Rooms

On **People booked**, choose **Rooms** to add rooms or change their names
and places. A room's places are a guide: the event's own places are the
limit. Set each person's room on the people booked list.

## Profit and loss

Open an event and choose **Profit and loss**:

- **Income** is what everyone booked owes in total (by option), plus money
  kept from cancelled bookings;
- **Costs** go in the spreadsheet at the bottom: room hire, food, goody bags
  and so on. Tick **Paid** once a cost is paid;
- **Profit** is income less costs, and **cash so far** is money received
  less costs paid.

**Reports** in the menu shows every event side by side, with totals, for one
year or all of them, and downloads as a CSV for your accountant. Copying an
event copies its costs too, as unpaid estimates to update.

## The catering sheet for the hotel

Open the event and choose **Printing and catering**, then **Catering
sheet**. It shows:

- how many people are on each option (the 4pm and 9am starts), the team, and
  what each option includes, for example Friday lunch;
- everyone's dietary needs, with their option and room.

Print it, save it as a PDF from the print window, or choose **Download as a
spreadsheet (CSV)** to email to the hotel. The dietary column comes from each
person's "Dietary and health" box, so it can include health information:
check it only shares what the hotel needs before you send it.

## Printing for the weekend

**Printing and catering** also has, each ready to print on A4:

- **Register:** everyone, the team first, with a box to tick as they arrive,
  their start time, room, dietary needs and anything they still owe;
- **Name badges:** eight to a page, with first names large;
- **Room lists:** who's in each room, one room to a page, for the doors;
- **Emergency contacts:** mobiles, emergency contacts and health needs. It's
  private: keep it with you and shred it after the event.

Choose **Print** at the top of each one. The menu and buttons don't print.

## Checking people in

Open the event and choose **Check in**. It's made for a phone at the door:

1. Start typing someone's name in **Find someone**.
2. Tap **Arrived**. It shows "Here" with the time.
3. Tapped the wrong person? Tap **Undo**.

The count at the top shows how many are here. Tick **Only people not here
yet** to see who's missing. Several of you can check people in at once: each
phone updates every 30 seconds. Owners and admins also see anything someone
still owes, to collect on the day; door staff don't see money.

Tip: add the site to your phone's home screen, so it opens like an app.
